Project background

About GOOD

What the platform is, who it's for, how access works, and how we ensure it outlives the grant.

The platform in one paragraph

The GOOD platform tackles a key barrier in space research: orbital dynamics software is often closed-source, fragmented, or domain-specific, and inaccessible to broader communities. As data quality, quantity, and diversity grow rapidly, this limits progress in domains ranging from space situational awareness — ensuring safe spacecraft operations — to planetary science — studying the origin and evolution of solar system bodies. By integrating Tudat's open-source orbital dynamics software with the WISE-based scalable data-handling system with complete data lineage, GOOD enables a paradigm shift in the field with fully transparent, open, and reproducible data and solution archiving across many applications.

User communities

Orbital dynamics is essential in numerous scientific domains, from planetary interior modelling and solar system orbital evolution to fundamental physics. It is also critical for safe and sustainable spacecraft operations and planetary defense. These diverse applications share a common methodology: determining quantities of interest — orbits, gravity fields, spacecraft properties — by fitting data to dynamical models.

Primary users of GOOD fall into three groups:

  • Data Analysis — researchers in solar system dynamics, planetary science, and SSA who need a transparent, reproducible analysis platform.
  • Data Acquisition — professionals and citizen scientists contributing observations to community-curated datasets.
  • Data Application — mission planners, space traffic managers, and planetary defense teams using validated estimation products.

Alignment with Open Science principles

Each analysis on the GOOD platform — data, outputs, and configurations — will be assigned persistent identifiers, ensuring reusability and findability. All database contents will be open, which is unique in this domain. Where they exist, the platform adopts standard data formats and protocols; otherwise, it collaboratively defines new ones. Translation layers will let GOOD interoperate with existing open- and closed-source tools.

"The platform allows the full community to validate and extend existing research, and enables diversification to new applications. It transforms orbital dynamics from a closed field into an accessible, collaborative effort."

Access policy

The platform will be globally accessible via both web- and terminal-based interfaces. Retrieval of existing analyses and archiving of user-generated analyses are always free under a Wide Access model. Hosted compute and configuration generation are free for non-commercial entities at low resource usage; intensive use follows a partial Excellence-driven model. Industrial users follow a Market-driven model with commercially viable fees — all reinvested into platform running costs, maintenance, and upgrades.

Governance

Governance balances openness, collaboration, and sustainability. A steering committee of core members manages project strategy; an advisory board of representatives from diverse fields ensures the platform's ambitions match community needs.

Sustainability

Tudat is a TU Delft 'laboratory facility' with dedicated maintenance funding since 2023. It supports more than a dozen funded projects and is central to the Spaceflight M.Sc. curriculum. WISE technology underpins the Euclid Science Ground Segment and is anticipated to be maintained by Euclid funding until at least 2033.

After the grant ends, the OmegaCEN team commits 0.5 FTE for platform maintenance. User fees, future research-grant budgets, and host-institute backstops provide layered financial security.

Software, licenses & citation

  • GOOD platform — GPL-based license, hosted on GitLab
  • Tudat — BSD 3-clause, hosted on GitHub at tudat-team
  • Astro-WISE Common libraries — GPL v3 (as part of the Euclid software stack)
  • Each stable release of GOOD will be assigned a persistent DOI via Zenodo
  • Both Tudat and WISE use Git for version control; conda packages for distribution
